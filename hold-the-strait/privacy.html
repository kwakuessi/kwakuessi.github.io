Hold the Strait ("the game") is made by [YOUR FULL NAME OR STUDIO NAME], based in Ghana ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the game on Android and iPhone.

Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Your progress: unlocked straits, stars, best scores, furthest wave, and your current saved game.
  • Your settings: sound and music on or off.

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. It stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from the game. The game has no sign-in, no chat, and no contact forms, and it does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA if you allow tracking).
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, and app version.
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Depending on your choices and where you live, the ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized (based on general context). Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell personal information for money. We receive only summary reports from AdMob (for example, the number of ads shown), not information that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game, you are asked whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices on the game's main menu.
  • iPhone: the game asks for permission to track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Everyone: you can manage the ads Google shows you at My Ad Center.
  • Watching rewarded ads in the game is always optional.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the game in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help.

Security and storage

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our servers. Information collected by Google is protected and stored under Google's policies, and may be processed in countries other than your own.

Since we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google using the links above. You are also welcome to contact us, and we will help where we can.
